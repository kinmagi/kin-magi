## Attribution

- Coursey, J. S.; Schwab, D. J.; Tsai, J. J.; Dragoset, R. A., NIST [Atomic Weights and Isotopic Compositions](https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-weights-and-isotopic-compositions-relative-atomic-masses), last data update January 2015. The compilation cites atomic weights 2013, isotopic compositions 2009 and AME2012 isotope masses. Selected elements: [H](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl?ele=H), [O](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl?ele=O), [Al](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl?ele=Al), [Cu](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl?ele=Cu). Representative abundance and uncertainty interpretation follows the [column notes](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Compositions/notes.html); these notes are older contextual documentation, not an assertion of a newer mass evaluation.
- NIST STAR composition records for [copper](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Star/compos.pl?matno=029), [aluminum](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Star/compos.pl?matno=013), and [liquid water](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Star/compos.pl?matno=276). These are nominal evaluation media. The [NIST X-ray evaluation table](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/XrayMassCoef/tab1.html) separately cautions about nominal densities; rounded values from it are not substituted for the STAR records.
- Linstrom, P. J. (editor), NIST Chemistry WebBook, SRD 69, 2025 data release, [copper thermochemistry](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7440508&Mask=2). Solid Shomate coefficients: Chase, M. W., Jr. (1998), NIST-JANAF Thermochemical Tables, Fourth Edition, J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data, Monograph 9, 1–1951; reviewed June 1977. [Rounded display table](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7440508&Mask=2&Table=on&Type=JANAFS) used only as a test oracle at a few temperatures.
- NIST WebBook [copper fusion temperature](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7440508&Mask=4), citing Anonymous (1988), NBS Spec. Publ. (U.S.) 260. Preserve its nominal-value description and TRC-assigned uncertainty without assuming a coverage factor.
- NIST [6061-T6 Aluminum (UNS A96061) cryogenic properties](https://trc.nist.gov/cryogenics/materials/6061%20Aluminum/6061_T6Aluminum_rev.htm), conductivity and specific-heat correlations. The [bibliography](https://trc.nist.gov/cryogenics/materials/references.htm), Aluminum 6061 section, lists Mann (1977) for specific heat and Veres / Touloukian for conductivity. This experiment uses published fits; it does not reproduce their underlying measurements.
- NIST [2022 CODATA constants](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt): c, Boltzmann constant, Avogadro constant, G and atomic mass constant. SI defining quantities retain exact-zero uncertainty; adjusted constants retain CODATA standard uncertainty.
- NIST [SP 811 Chapter 4](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-chapter-4-two-classes-si-units-and-si-prefixes) for dimensions, SI prefixes and Celsius offset; [Appendix B.8](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b8) for unit conversion factors. Historical constant definitions in SP 811 are not used in place of CODATA 2022.
- NIST [Kilogram: Mass and Planck's Constant](https://www.nist.gov/si-redefinition/kilogram/kilogram-mass-and-plancks-constant) for the established mass-energy relation used by the pure rest-energy query. No mass-energy conversion operation is applied to world states.
